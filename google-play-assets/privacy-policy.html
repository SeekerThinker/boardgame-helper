桌游助手 / Board Game Assistant

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy · 更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-07-22

本应用不需要账号，不使用广告或分析服务，也不会把玩家名称、计时、分数或随机工具结果发送到外部服务器。

数据与本地存储

玩家名称、计时设置、桌游局进度、计分历史和工具结果仅保存在你的设备上。卸载应用或清除站点数据会删除这些记录。

通知权限

如果你主动启用“后台到时提醒”，应用会请求系统通知权限，并仅在正在运行的计时器到期时创建本地通知。通知不经过远程服务器，也不包含追踪标识。

联网与第三方服务

原生应用可离线运行，不接入账号、广告、分析或云同步 SDK。Web/PWA 版本只会从你访问的网站下载应用静态文件。

儿童隐私

应用不收集任何人的个人信息，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

你的选择

你可以在系统设置中关闭通知，也可以通过重置桌游局、清除浏览器站点数据或卸载应用删除本地记录。

联系

如需提交隐私咨询，请使用本应用商店商品页中公开的“开发者联系方式”或“App 支持”渠道。该联系方式由发布者在商店后台维护。

Data and local storage

Player names, timer settings, game progress, score history, and tool results are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes these records.

Notification permission

If you enable Background Timer Alerts, the app asks for notification permission and creates a local notification only when an active timer expires. Notifications do not pass through a remote server and contain no tracking identifiers.

Network and third parties

The native app works offline and includes no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud-sync SDK. The Web/PWA version only downloads static app files from the website you visit.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can disable notifications in system settings and delete local records by resetting the game, clearing browser site data, or uninstalling the app.

Contact

To submit a privacy inquiry, use the public Developer Contact or App Support channel on this app's store listing. The publisher maintains that contact information in the store console.
