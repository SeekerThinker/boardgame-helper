## 中文教学草稿

**目标：** 两人同时给出三种选项之一，靠相克关系赢得这一局；也可以约定先赢三局的一方获胜。

**准备：** 面对面或能同时听见彼此的两位玩家，先约好听到“三”才一起出手。握拳代表石头、摊开的手代表布、两根伸出的手指代表剪刀。不想比手势时可约好用口头同步报出。

**一局这样进行：**
1. 两人共同数到三，同时亮出选项；不要等看到对方后再更改。
2. 选项相同算平局，这局不计胜负，再出一次。
3. 石头胜剪刀，剪刀胜布，布胜石头；分别选了不同选项时依此记一胜。
4. 只玩一局就结束；玩多局时预先约定先获几胜，不要在过程中改目标。

**例子（原创编写，非来源原文）：** 小岚出布，小航出石头；小岚得到这一局的一胜。下一局两人都出剪刀，不计胜负，重新一起出手。

**参与提示：** 不要求接触对方；同时出手的节奏可以先练习。口头替代必须由双方事先同意；多人可分成两人一组轮流玩，但本条目录人数仍只记 2。

## English teaching draft

**Goal:** Reveal one of three choices at the same moment. The matching rule decides who takes that round; agree on a target such as three wins if you want a short match.

**Setup:** Two players agree to reveal together on a count of three. A closed fist means rock, an open palm means paper, and two extended fingers mean scissors. Both may agree to speak their choices simultaneously instead if gestures are inaccessible.

**Round:**
1. Count together and show one choice at the same time; do not change it after seeing the other hand.
2. Identical choices make a tie, so replay the round without awarding a point.
3. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock.
4. Stop after one decisive round or at the win target you agreed on before starting.

**Original example:** Alex shows paper while Bo shows rock, giving Alex one round. Both then show scissors: the next round is a tie and is replayed.

**Participation:** No physical contact is needed. Practise the reveal rhythm first; any spoken-choice alternative needs both players’ consent and practical checking.
