## 中文教学草稿

**目标：** 其他玩家在总共二十次提问内找出一个事先想好的答案。

**准备：** 选一位回答者，让 TA 在心里决定一个大家可能认识的具体物品，例如日常生活用品。告诉其他人答案属于哪个大类，但不说答案本身。约定每句直接猜答案也算一次提问；本教程只允许回答“是”或“否”，含糊的问题先改写后再计数。

**进行：**
1. 提问者按顺序轮流问一句只能用“是”或“否”回答的问题。
2. 回答者根据预先决定的答案如实回答，大家共同记住已经排除的范围；总次数每发出一个有效问题就增加一。
3. 有人认为知道答案时，可以用一次提问直接猜；猜对则立即结束。
4. 到第二十问仍没人猜对，回答者说出答案。这一轮结束，换人想一个新答案。

**原创例子：** 答案是“雨伞”。有人问“通常在下雨时用它吗？”，回答“是”；再问“它能折叠吗？”，须按照回答者一开始心中那把伞的实际设定回答。这个例子用于展示先明确答案范围的重要性，非来源讲义示例。

**参与提示：** 不需接触或站立；先选所有人熟悉的答案，避免把个人隐私、冷僻知识当作唯一答案。八人以上也可以玩，只是等待时间会变长，本教程人数上限不是传统规则。

## English teaching draft

**Goal:** Work out a hidden answer using no more than twenty yes-or-no questions in total.

**Setup:** Pick an answer keeper. They silently choose one everyday object familiar to the group, and may announce a broad category without revealing it. Agree that a direct guess uses up one question. Rephrase ambiguous questions before counting them; this draft uses only yes or no as replies.

**Play:**
1. The other players take turns asking a question that can be answered yes or no.
2. The keeper answers consistently with the answer chosen at the start. Count each valid question toward the shared limit of twenty.
3. Anyone may spend a question on a direct guess. A correct guess ends the round.
4. If the twentieth question does not reveal the answer, the keeper tells everyone what it was. Choose a different keeper for the next round.

**Original example:** The answer is an umbrella. A question about use in rain gets a yes; a question about folding depends on the particular umbrella the keeper had in mind. Agree on specificity to avoid contradictory replies.

**Participation:** Sitting is fine, and no contact is necessary. Text or written questions are possible adaptations, but would change the zero-equipment requirement and need real-world testing.
