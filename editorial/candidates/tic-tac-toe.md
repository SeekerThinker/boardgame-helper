## 中文教学草稿

**目标：** 比对方先把自己的三个符号连成一条直线。

**准备：** 在纸上画两条横线、两条竖线，得到九个位置。两人分别选不同的记号（例如圆和叉），约定谁先下；每个位置只能填一个记号。

**进行：**
1. 轮到的玩家在空白格写下自己的记号。
2. 每次落子后检查三行、三列和两条对角线：谁先让自己三个记号排成其中一条完整直线，谁赢得这一盘，立即停止。
3. 若九格都被填满还没有三连线，这盘算平局。
4. 再玩一盘时擦掉或另画新棋盘，可交换先手；多盘的胜负可自行记分。

**原创例子（用坐标而非复制任何棋谱）：** 把左上角叫 A1、中上角叫 B1、右上角叫 C1。若同一玩家先后占了 A1、B1 和 C1 且此前无人已经连线，那么落下 C1 时该玩家获胜。

**参与提示：** 不需要移动整个身体。若九格太小，可以画大一些；双方确认记号清楚可辨。勿在未经允许的桌面或墙面上涂写。

## English teaching draft

**Goal:** Make a straight line of three of your own marks before the other player does.

**Setup:** Draw a three-by-three grid on paper. Choose two different marks, such as a circle and a cross. Agree who starts; each square can contain only one mark.

**Play:**
1. Take turns placing your mark in an empty square.
2. After each turn, check the three rows, three columns and two diagonals. The first player to complete any one of those lines wins immediately.
3. If all nine squares are occupied without a winning line, the board is a draw.
4. For another board, redraw the grid and optionally swap the starting player; keep score only if you want a longer match.

**Original example:** Label the three top squares A1, B1 and C1 from left to right. If one player completes all three before any earlier winning line has ended the board, their mark at C1 finishes a winning top row.

**Participation:** A larger grid and strongly contrasting marks can help visibility. A helper may draw on a player's instructions by agreement; check the adaptation with actual users before publication.
